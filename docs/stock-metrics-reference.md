# Single Stock Analysis — Key Metrics Reference

> Thresholds are starting points. **Sector context overrides absolute values.**
> Always benchmark against industry peers, not universal cutoffs.

---

## Profitability & Growth

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Sign | Caution | Red Flag | Impact on Stock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue Growth | Business expansion | >10–15% consistent | 0–10% | Negative | Drives future earnings | Consistency matters more than a single year spike |
| EPS Growth | Profit per share growth | >15% | 5–15% | Negative | Major long-term driver | Verify it's not from buybacks alone |
| Free Cash Flow | Cash after capex | Positive & growing | Flat | Negative | Supports growth/dividends | Harder to manipulate than earnings |
| FCF Margin | Cash efficiency | >15% | 5–15% | <5% | High quality economics | FCF / Revenue; prefer this over net margin for capex-heavy firms |
| FCF Yield | Cash return on price | >5% | 2–5% | <2% | Valuation efficiency | FCF / Market Cap; complements P/E |
| Gross Margin | Pricing power | High & stable | Stable | Declining | Competitive advantage | Sector-relative; software >60%, retail 20–30% is normal |
| Operating Margin | Operational efficiency | >20% | 8–20% | <5% | Business strength | Grocery/logistics structurally run near 2–5% — use peers |
| Net Margin | Final profitability | >15% tech / >10% general | 5–10% | <5% | Supports valuation | Some excellent businesses (retail, distribution) run 2–4% by design |

## Returns on Capital

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Sign | Caution | Red Flag | Impact on Stock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ROE | Equity efficiency | >15–20% | 10–15% | <10% | Management quality | **Watch for leverage distortion** — high debt inflates ROE artificially; pair with D/E |
| ROIC | True capital returns | >15% or >WACC | Near WACC | <WACC | Value creation | Best single measure of capital efficiency; ROIC > WACC = value creation |

## Valuation

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Sign | Caution | Red Flag | Impact on Stock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P/E Ratio | Price vs earnings | 15–25 (value/stable) | Industry avg +20% | >40 with no growth | Shows market expectations | Meaningless without growth context; high P/E can be justified by high growth |
| PEG Ratio | Growth-adjusted P/E | <1.0 | 1.0–1.5 | >2.0 | Growth vs valuation | P/E ÷ EPS growth rate; normalizes P/E for growth |
| EV/EBITDA | Enterprise valuation | <10–12 | 12–15 | >18–20 | Most useful for mature/capital-heavy firms | Better than P/E for comparing firms with different capital structures |
| P/B Ratio | Price vs book assets | <1.5 | 1.5–3 | >3 | Relevant for banks & asset-heavy firms | **Largely irrelevant for asset-light businesses** (software, platforms) — skip for these |

## Financial Health & Risk

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Sign | Caution | Red Flag | Impact on Stock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Debt/Equity | Financial leverage | <0.5–1 | 1–2 | >2 | Risk during downturns | Context matters: utilities/REITs carry structural debt; compare within sector |
| Interest Coverage | Debt payment safety | >10× | 3–10× | <2× | Survival indicator | EBIT ÷ Interest expense; <2× means earnings barely cover interest |
| Current Ratio | Short-term liquidity | >1.5 | 1.0–1.5 | <1.0 | Liquidity strength | Some asset-light businesses run <1.0 intentionally (negative working capital) |

---

## Shareholder Value

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Sign | Caution | Red Flag | Impact on Stock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Share Dilution | New shares issued | Shrinking (buybacks) | Slow increase | Rapid increase | Reduces per-share value | Check share count trend over 5+ years |
| Dividend Payout Ratio | Dividend sustainability | 20–60% | 60–80% | >90% | Income sustainability | **Exception: REITs & MLPs are legally required to pay 90%+** — normal for those structures |
| Insider Ownership | Management alignment | >5–10% | 1–5% | <1% or heavy selling | Skin in the game | Insider buying > selling is a positive signal |

## Qualitative Risk Factors

These don't have clean ratios but materially affect single-stock risk:

| Factor | Watch For |
|---|---|
| Customer concentration | Any single customer >20% of revenue is a concentration risk |
| Revenue per employee | Productivity signal; compare to sector peers |
| Recurring vs one-time revenue | Subscription/recurring = more predictable; project-based = lumpier |
| Moat indicators | Switching costs, network effects, cost advantages, intangible assets |
| Management track record | Capital allocation history, insider ownership trend |

## Quick Sector Adjustments

| Sector | Metrics to Emphasize | Metrics to Discount |
|---|---|---|
| Software / SaaS | FCF Margin, Revenue Growth, Gross Margin, NRR | P/B, EV/EBITDA (early stage) |
| Banks / Financials | P/B, ROE, Net Interest Margin | EBITDA-based metrics |
| Retail / Distribution | Same-store sales growth, Inventory turns, Operating Margin (sector-relative) | P/B, Net Margin absolute |
| Industrials / Manufacturing | ROIC, EV/EBITDA, Free Cash Flow | High gross margin expectations |
| REITs | FFO (Funds from Operations), P/FFO, Debt/Assets | Standard EPS, Payout Ratio |
| Energy | EV/EBITDA, FCF Yield, Reserve replacement | P/E (earnings volatile with commodity prices) |
